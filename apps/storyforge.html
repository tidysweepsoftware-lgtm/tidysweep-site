What it is

A story studio that turns your idea into a finished video

StoryForge is a Windows app for people who run story channels on YouTube. You write the idea for an episode in your own words: who the people are, what happens and how it ends. StoryForge turns it into a complete narrated video, with a script, a voice, a picture for every moment, captions and music.

Making one story video by hand takes days: writing, recording, finding or drawing pictures, then editing it all together. StoryForge does those slow parts for you, step by step, and you can look at and change every step before it goes on to the next.

The AI that writes, speaks and draws runs on your own graphics card, not on someone else's server. Your stories stay on your PC, and there is no fee for every video you make.

How it works

From start to finish in 6 steps

Write your idea

Describe the story in your own words: the people, the events and the ending you want. As short or as detailed as you like.

StoryForge plans it

Your idea is laid out as a story plan: the parts, who is in each one, when and where it happens, and who knows which secret.

The script is written

StoryForge writes the narration point by point, following your plan, so the story stays the one you meant to tell.

It is read aloud

A natural voice narrates the script, with the pauses and pace a storyteller would use.

Every moment gets a picture

Pictures are drawn in the style you pick, with character sheets that keep every face the same.

Edit, export and post

Check it on the timeline, add captions and music, export the video and take the title, description, chapters and thumbnail with you.

A closer look

Everything StoryForge offers

Your idea stays in charge

Many AI writers drift away from what you asked for. StoryForge works the other way round: your idea becomes the plan, and the script is written point by point against that plan.

It keeps track of the facts of your story, such as when each part happens, where it takes place, who is there and who knows which secret, so people do not suddenly know things they should not.

A voice that tells the story

Natural narration voices read the whole script. StoryForge places the pauses a storyteller uses, between moments and before the big reveals, so the narration does not sound like a machine reading a list.

A picture for every moment

Each moment of the script gets its own picture, drawn in the look you choose: cartoon, real life, anime, manhwa, watercolor and more.

Character sheets describe how every person looks, so the same face, hair and clothes come back in every picture. You can also train your own style from pictures you like.

Edit it like a video editor

The finished episode opens on a timeline. Move pictures, change timings, add captions, effects and music, and export a video file that is ready for YouTube.

Ready to publish

For every episode StoryForge suggests titles, writes a description with chapters and helps you make a thumbnail, so the upload takes minutes, not an evening.

Questions

Good to know

Do I need to be a writer?

No. A few lines about who is in the story and what happens are enough. The more you say, the closer the result follows you.

Can I change the script before the video is made?

Yes. Every step can be checked and changed: the plan, the script, the voice and every picture.

Does it work without the internet?

The AI runs on your own PC, so your stories are not sent to an online service while you work.

What kind of stories can I make?

Real-life drama, revenge and justice, love stories, mystery, fantasy, and anime or manhwa style stories, among others.
